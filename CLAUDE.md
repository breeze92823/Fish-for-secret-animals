# Fish for Secret Animals

Built on the "Throw Food for Baddies" engine and turned into a fishing game. **Naming:** the code
keeps the engine's internal names so nothing had to be rewired: a "food" is a **rod** (`FOODS`,
`S.food`, `foods.js`), a "throw" is a **cast** (`throw.js`, `THROW`, `THROW_MODE`), a "baddie" /
"girl" is an **animal** (`BADDIES`, `Baddie`, `S.baddies`, `AREAS[i].girls`, `SEASON_GIRLS`), the
"lane" is the **river**. Everything the player sees says rod / cast / animal / river.

## Repos
- Frontend (this repo): `C:\ThreeJS\Fish-for-secret-animals\Fish-for-secret-animals`
- Backend: `C:\ThreeJS\Fish-for-secret-animals\Fish-for-secret-animals-backend` (Colyseus + MongoDB; its CLAUDE.md lists the message contract and which constants must stay in step with `config.js`). The frontend uses it for **bases**, the **server-side save** (`saveGame` / `game`, see "Progress is saved on the server only"), presence, the Daily / Spin / River Giant events, rewarded-ad verification, invites and the leaderboards (`BOARD_BOTS` is only the offline stand-in). Local dev: the `backend` + `net-test` entries in `..\.claude\launch.json`.
- **Bases / room size**: the hub has `PENS.xs.length` (5) bases and a room holds exactly that many players (backend `BASE_COUNT` = `ROOM_MAX_CLIENTS` = the `roomSize` sent to Bloxity on deploy; keep all in step). At boot `main.js` joins the room (`net.connect()`, through the Bloxity matchmaker as the hosting docs require: `Legion.SDK.net.resolveEndpoint`, a new Client per resolve, retry with backoff up to ~60 s) and the server assigns this player a base index (`PlayerState.base`). `myPen = pens[base]`, `setSpawn()` (player.js) puts the player in front of it, the other bases show who sits there (`Pen.setOccupant`: name + picture) or "Free base". A dropped room is rejoined asking for the same base; a different one reloads the page (`net.onRebase`). With no server (no SDK and no `VITE_SERVER_URL`, or nobody answers) the game plays offline on the middle pen with the `NEIGHBOURS` stand-ins. Local backend: set `VITE_SERVER_URL=ws://localhost:2567` in `.env`.
- Run: the `fish` entry in `..\.claude\launch.json` (vite dev on 5261, `--mode development`, `VITE_DEV_MODE=true`).

## The game
A lego-style hub (sunken stud meadow ringed by two tiers of grass-topped dirt cliffs). Five fenced pens line the south side; the middle one is yours. North of the meadow the yellow road runs east-west; the river canyon opens north between the leaderboards (west wall) and the Sell stall, Fuse Machine and free chest (east). **The river** (`HUB.lane`: banks `floorY` = 10 m below the hub, a channel `river` m either side of the centre, `depth` m deep, the water surface `water` m below the banks; `makeRiver` in hub/world.js): grassy banks along both walls carry each area's props, the channel has a sandy stud bed with kelp, rocks and shells, and one animated stylised water surface per area (cell-caustic shader, `AREAS[i].water` / `waterLine`; lava glows), lily pads, and a rope of buoys at every area border.
**Loop** (game/throw.js): step onto the yellow CAST band at the river mouth: the camera swings behind you, rod out (`rodMesh`), the bobber hangs from the tip on the line; a power meter sweeps; tap (click / Space / F / E / touch) to stop it (PERFECT/GREAT/GOOD/WEAK multiplies luck); **the cast show** (`THROW_MODE.castFx`, one entry per grade): the big per-letter banner (`ui.castShow`: "PERFECT CAST!" rainbow, GREAT lime, GOOD gold, WEAK orange and drooping; a Jackpot Bait makes it "SECRET CAST! GUARANTEED: SECRET", a Lucky Cast "LUCKY CAST!") with a bonus line, a gold / silver screen frame (speed rays, grid, glowing edges) and flat confetti; PERFECT / GREAT cut to a hero shot (`TM.cam.hero`, front-left of you) while you jump into a longer windup with the rod overhead and the left arm out (`thrower.hop()` / `armLAngle()`, applied in main.js), then a lunge and 3D rainbow confetti cubes at the release; sounds `sfx.castShow` (fanfare stabs + poppers + riser .. sad trombone) and `sfx.castRelease`; the banner flies up as the LUCK counter drops in; the rod swings back and whips forward, the camera chases the bobber down the river (line paying out, speed lines, the LUCK counter climbs with distance, each area slams its name), slow-mo splashdown (white voxel cubes, rings, foam), the bobber plunges and bobs; **the bite** (`THROW_MODE.bite`): nibbles dip the bobber, then the camera dives underwater (main.js tints the fog while `thrower.underwater`) and we see **through the animal's eyes** (`THROW_MODE.bite.pov`, `povShot` in throw.js, `ui.pov*` overlay: ANIMAL VIEW tag, lens ring, focus brackets, "!" and thought captions): wide-angle search sweep, sonar ping + FOOD! when it spots the bait (swollen and glinting), dolly-zoom approach with a heartbeat that speeds up (`sfx.dive / sonar / heartbeat / lunge / chomp`), a lunge with an FOV punch and shake, then a flash cuts out to the third-person chomp (the animal stays hidden until then); back on top the bobber is yanked under, then the animal **bursts out of the water** in a splash of white cubes and floats; its name types out before the rarity card ($/s, NEW!, PEN FULL!); continue and it spins into the **hotbar** (or swims off if the hotbar is full). Back in the hub, select a hotbar slot to carry it; inside your pen click (E / F, touch PLACE) to place it; only placed animals earn $/s. Exit (Esc / Q) leaves; Auto plays the loop; the Lucky Cast (100x luck for one cast) costs money, priced from your income. Training at the pull-up bar earns XP; training levels give **Power** (strength). **Cast range** = the rod's base distance (`dist`, 20 m x1.08 a tier) x (1 + strength x `THROW.strengthPct`, capped at +`strengthCap`). **Pacing**: rod prices (`FOOD_LIST`), `TRAINING.need` / `benches`, `AREA_INCOME` and `THROW` were tuned together (last area in about 2 hours); change them together. Money buys rods (49 tiers Common .. Exotic plus the Galaxy Rod, `FOODS` / `FOOD_RARITIES`; each rod is a procedural voxel rod coloured from its `colors` [blank, grip, accent] with more trim per rarity, and its bobber is in the accent colour; `foodMesh` = shop portrait, `rodMesh` = in hand, `bobberMesh` = on the line), pen capacity, Shop upgrades. 3 of a kind fuse into BIG (x4), 3 BIG into HUGE (x20). **Nothing costs premium currency** (see the root CLAUDE.md).
**Areas** (`AREAS`, mirrored as z ranges in `HUB.zones`): Lily Pond, Farm Creek, Willow Lake, Desert Oasis, Frozen River, Maple Stream, Crystal Falls, Sunset Bay, Coral Reef, Cloud Springs, Lava Lake, Cosmic Sea (ids meadow .. cosmic are kept). Each has bank colours, its own bank props (`cover`), water colours, particles (`fx`) and **ten original animals** (made up; never copy existing characters): `{ n, k, c, c2, c3, x }` = name, body plan, main / second / accent colour, extras. A slot sets rarity (`RARITY_SLOTS` + one step every three areas), odds (`SLOT_ODDS`) and income (`AREA_INCOME` x `SLOT_INCOME`). Rarities: Common .. Secret plus Divine, Celestial, Eternal.
**Animals** (`game/baddieModel.js`, class still `Baddie`): voxel models painted with the rods' Vox + mesher (`foods.js` exports `Vox`, `meshVox`). 17 body plans (`KINDS`: duck, frog, fish, turtle, crab, penguin, seal, otter, octopus, jelly, shark, dolphin, whale, axolotl, swan, croc, seahorse), each split into parts around pivots (body, head, fl/fr front limbs, bl/br back limbs, tail, wl/wr wings) so they waddle, flap, wag, nod, hop and swim; extras: crown, halo, horn, wings, spots, stripes, spikes, tusks, whiskers, straw, shiny, round, glow (accent voxels bloom). Sized by height (long animals count 3/4 of their length). Swimmers hover on land (`ground`), the throw sets `ground = false` in the water. Modes: idle / walk / dance / cheer / eat / feed / fly / swim / float + the APPEAL moves (cues for pens.js). Vox coordinates are rounded in `put` (fractional coords used to corrupt the key).
**Look** (matches the reference): the default mood (`TIME_OF_DAY.day`) is a twilight: deep indigo zenith, violet middle, a red-orange sunset glow + low sun disc at the far (north) end of the river (`discAzimuth` 180), orange-lit cloud streaks, a faint nebula, dot + sparkle stars; the key light comes from behind the hub camera (`sunAzimuth`) in warm peach with a lavender / peach hemi fill so everything stays bright; violet-pink distance fog. N switches to a moonlit night. The sky shader (lighting.js) takes zenith / mid / horizon / glowColor / cloudLit / cloudDark / clouds / nebula; `skyTint` tints the 3D puff clouds (world.js). Canyon dirt is rust-red (`paintCliff` defaults), the water is self-lit teal so it stays vivid under the warm light.
**UI look** (matches the reference; the "Fishing look" blocks at the end of hud.css / throwUi.css override the older rules): the left menu is icons with an outlined label under each (two columns below 760 px tall, so it never runs into the stats), the quest is big white outlined text with the reward in gold, Power reads "10 Power". While aiming the cast: black letterbox bars, a ruled meter, a rainbow Lucky Cast tile, Power in the bottom bar (`ui.setPower`). The luck counter is gold numbers over a dark band with the area name. The reveal has no card: the name in huge outlined letters with a tilted NEW! tag (`.new-tag` inside the name), the rarity in its colour, $/s in green, then "click to continue"; the variant / season stamp sits above the name and HOTBAR FULL! under the income; Celestial+ names cycle through rainbow colours.
The yellow cast band (`buildThrowBand` in hub/props.js, `THROW_MODE.band`) sits just south of the barrier. Yellow chevrons lead to the current quest target (`questTarget` in main.js).

## Live events, rewards and collection extras
- **Variants** (`VARIANTS`: GOLD x2 1/20, DIAMOND x5 1/200, RAINBOW x15 1/2000): rolled on every catch in `throw.js rollMeet` (featured area x3, luck helps a little); stored as `v` on pen / hotbar entries; tinted body + ring in `baddieModel.js` (`variant` option, rainbow cycles), stamps on cards / hotbar / reveal card. Fusing needs girl + size + variant to match (`state.fuseKey`). **Sizes** go up to GIANT (x100) and TITAN (x500).
- **Events** (`game/events.js`, all on the UTC clock, no server): Luck Hour (first 10 min of every hour, luck x2), Weekend (Sat/Sun income x2), Featured area (rotates every 30 min: luck x1.5, variants x3), the **River Giant** (`game/boss.js`, at :20 and :50 for 4 min: a giant season animal surfaces at the river end; the room server counts everyone's casts, backend `boss.ts`; offline the client counts its own), and **Seasons** (`SEASONS`, 14 days each, rotating: 4 season girls each, `SEASON_GIRLS`, area -1, entry `a` = the area she was caught in sets her income; 1 in `SEASON.chance` throws; Event Tickets per throw / per season girl; the event shop `EVENT_SHOP` sells potions, Lucky Throws and Season Lures for tickets only). HUD pills over the money (`ui/eventsUi.js`) and the Events window.
- **Rewards window** (`ui/rewards.js`): playtime gifts (`PLAYTIME_GIFTS`, per session), Daily Reward (`DAILY`, server clock via backend `daily.ts`: `claimDaily` -> `dailyResult`), spin wheel (`SPIN`, server rolls via `spin` -> `spinResult`, ids = backend `SPIN_WEDGES`; an unknown id pays the first wedge). Offline both run locally for the session. Prizes go through `state.grantPrize` (cash = secs of income, Lucky Throws, potions `S.potions`, tickets).
- **Throw extras**: PERFECT streak (`STREAK`, +10% luck per PERFECT in a row, GOOD/WEAK resets), distance records + milestone cash (`RECORDS`), `S.stats.bestArea/bestDist/bestStreak/bestVariant/bestRarity`.
- **Quests** (`QUESTS`, ~42): `goal` quests complete themselves (`state.checkQuests`, twice a second); `secs` rewards scale with income (`state.questReward`). **Index rewards** (`INDEX_REWARD`): a finished page pays cash + a title (`S.titles`).
- **Pacing**: all of the above adds money, so food prices are the tuned per-tier list x2.5; the progression sim still puts a throw-and-train player at the last area in about 2 hours. Change rewards and prices together.

Three.js (vanilla, no React) + Vite + Rapier (`@dimforge/rapier3d-compat`, wasm inlined; `main.js` awaits its init at top level, hence `build.target: 'es2022'`). Everything is procedural (canvas textures); the only imported model is the Bloxity base rig `public/avatars/player.glb`. Font: Fredoka (loaded in `index.html`).

## Mobile & responsive (required for every change)
The game must play on phones and tablets (portrait and landscape) as well as desktop. Treat a feature as unfinished until it works on touch and on a small screen.
- **Input**: every action needs a touch path, not just a key. Use Pointer Events (`pointerdown/move/up`, check `e.pointerType`), never mouse-only events. Layout (implemented in `src/touch.js`): left-thumb virtual joystick -> `inputState.move`, right-side drag -> `inputState.look`, on-screen buttons for jump/sprint/mode/etc., pinch -> `inputState.zoom`. Everything still feeds `inputState` in `src/input.js` so game code stays input-agnostic. Pointer lock does not exist on touch; never rely on it.
- **Detection**: decide touch UI with `matchMedia('(pointer: coarse)')` / `navigator.maxTouchPoints`, not the user agent. Show touch controls only on coarse pointers; a device can have both.
- **Layout/UI**: HUD and menus use relative units (`%`, `vw/vh`, `dvh`, `clamp()`) and flexbox/grid, no fixed pixel layouts. Tap targets >= 44px. Respect notches with `env(safe-area-inset-*)` (viewport already has `viewport-fit=cover`). Text readable at 360px width. No hover-only interactions. Add `touch-action: none` on the canvas and game controls so the browser does not scroll/zoom.
- **Resize/orientation**: anything sized to the screen (camera aspect, renderer, HUD) must react to `resize` (covers orientation changes). Use `visualViewport` where the on-screen keyboard or browser bars matter.
- **Performance**: mobile GPUs are weak. Respect the `graphics_quality` presets in `src/config.js` (cap DPR, shadow map size, draw distance); default to a lower preset on coarse-pointer devices. Avoid per-frame allocations; keep draw calls and texture sizes low.
- **Testing**: check every UI/input change at a phone viewport (e.g. 375x812 and landscape 812x375) with touch emulation, plus desktop, before calling it done.

## Multiplayer presence (net.js, remote.js)
Everything below rides on the server roster (backend `PlayerState`) plus two relayed events; offline play is unchanged.
- `main.js sendPresence`: pose (~10 Hz, only on change + 1 s heartbeat: position, yaw, grounded, vy, right-arm angle, lean, `act` aim/train, equipped food, carried baddie, income), `pen` (placed baddies, 2 Hz signature), `setAvatar` (Bloxity look JSON `{equipped, proportions}`), `throw` (from throw.js `onThrow`: food, from, to, dur, peak), `emote`.
- `remote.js`: one `RemotePlayer` per other player: Bloxity skin / accessories / proportions (`attachEquippedAccessories` + `bloxity.skinTextureUrlFor`), the procedural animator (idle / run / jump), arm poses, the food in hand while aiming, the baddie carried in the arms, name tag, SDK chat bubbles, Bloxity emotes (`avatar/emotes.js`, clips from `GET {apiUrl}/v1/avatar/emotes`) and the replayed throw (`Flight`: same arc, trail, landing burst). Cheap by design: `REMOTE` in config (animator at a third of the rate beyond `farDist`, nothing drawn beyond `hideDist`, particles only within `fxDist`).
- **One unit per player**: base + Training Place. `placeUnit(base)` (config.js) shifts `TRAINING.spot` beside the assigned base before `main.js` builds it; only your own training station exists (other bases show just the pen + occupant). The Rod / Bait / Trail shops are shared, by the leaderboards. After a girl lands in the hotbar the throw ends and `throw.js leaveToBase()` respawns you at your base (Auto mode still loops back to the meter).
- `Pen.syncRemote(str, income)`: another player's base shows their placed baddies (the existing wandering / appeal animations and particles), income and name; `Pen.setOccupant` the name + picture or "Free base".
- Bloxity SDK (`bloxity.js`): `registerFeature('emotes')` (NOT `play_with_friends`: hosted games get CrazyGames' Play with friends free through the matchmaker; that call is only for games that handle `?instantMultiplayer=true` themselves), `party.enable({ maxPartySize: bases })` + `party.joined`, `game.updateRoom` (`relay~room`), `?roomId=` launch join (`net.js joinLaunchRoom`), `playerJoined / playerInRoom` (remote.js `sync`: each other player is reported once, as soon as their name is known; those present when we joined or rejoined (first roster of a session, 3 s window) go through `playerInRoom`, later arrivals through `playerJoined`; these only drive the portal's friend-join toasts, the SDK has no call that fills the Esc menu with a player list), chat bubbles, emote events, ad breaks (pause + mute; screens declared but off, no rewarded ads), leaderboards (`LEADERBOARDS`: submit every 30 s, top lists replace `BOARD_BOTS` when the SDK answers), settings `master_volume` / `music_volume`, friend boost = Bloxity friends in your room (`state.setFriends`), tap the FRIEND BOOST badge to copy an invite link. No gems / purchases (see the root CLAUDE.md).
- Dev: `launch.json` entry `net-test` (vite on 5252 with `VITE_SERVER_URL=ws://localhost:2567`) + the backend (`npm start` there). Browser tabs in the background do not run the render loop, so they stop sending poses: front the tab you are testing. `__game` exposes `net` and `remote`.

## Progress is saved on the server only
- `game/state.js` has no browser storage: `hydrate(doc)` loads the server's document at boot (`net.loadGame()`, waits for the backend's `game` message; `serverError` / timeout = play without saving so a save that could not be read is never overwritten) and `save()` (debounced `ECONOMY.saveEveryMs`, plus on tab hide / unload) sends the whole `S` through `net.saveGame` -> backend `saveGame` (bounded by `game.ts sanitizeGame`, stored as the `game` field of the player's Mongo doc). The Index "NEW" tags live in `S.seen`. Offline, as a guest (never stored) or with no database the session just is not stored. Only device preferences stay in the browser (sound volume / mute, the Active panel fold).
- A new field in `S`: add it to `fresh()` AND to backend `GameSave` / `sanitizeGame`, or the server drops it.
- Rewarded ads (`ads.js`, `ADS` in config): Free Gift button (only while `bloxity.adsAvailable()`; simulated in `npm run dev` without the SDK) -> `bloxity.showRewarded(placement)` -> only on 'completed' the server verifies the token (`net.verifyAd` -> backend `adReward` -> Bloxity `/v1/ads/reward/verify`) -> reward (2x income for 10 min = `S.adBoostUntil`, a cash boost, a free Lucky Throw = `S.luckyCredits`). Wins / money only. Gameplay banners: `bloxity.setAdScreen('gameplay' | 'panel' | null)` each 0.5 s (none during throws, training, ad breaks), declared with `declareScreens({ gameplay: { show: true } })`; only the two right-hand corners are left free for banners.

## Rooms and leaderboards on Bloxity
- Open Rooms needs the backend's room reporter (see its CLAUDE.md) plus the client's `updateRoom` (`net.js reportRoom`, `relay~room`) and `?roomId=` join (`net.js joinLaunchRoom` -> `bloxity.locateRoom`).
- The client sends its Bloxity token (`bloxity.getToken()`) on join and `identify`: on a deployed server it is the only way to be a signed-in player (progress saved, leaderboards).
- Plaza boards: the server's `leaderboard` message (`net.onLeaderboard`, `LEADERBOARDS[i].stat`) first, then Bloxity's top list, then the offline stand-ins. Bloxity boards: `LEADERBOARDS` in config (same ids / settings as the backend; permanent once declared).
- `BLOXITY_CODE_METADATA.md` answers Bloxity's SDK-integration audit questions: keep it current.

## Ads policy and the friend invite boost
- No ads while the player plays: `gameplay` banners are declared off and never reported; `bloxity.setAdScreen` only reports `'panel'` while a HUD window is open. Rewarded ads stay opt-in (Free Gift button). Midroll ad breaks are the platform's: the game only pauses and mutes for them (`onAdBreak`).
- Friend invite boost (`ECONOMY.inviteBoost` +10% per invited friend, up to `inviteMax` 10, permanent; `state.setInvites` / `inviteBoostPct`, added to the friends-in-room boost in `incomeMult`): tapping the FRIEND BOOST badge copies `bloxity.inviteLink(roomKey, myUid)` (the SDK link with `ref` = your verified account id). The friend opens the game with `?ref=`, the client sends it on join (`bloxity.launchRef`), the server credits you (backend `creditInvite`), the count comes with the `game` message and live as `invites` (`net.onInvites`).

## Tackle: rods, baits, trails, boosts (all money, never premium currency)
- **Windows** (`ui/tackleShop.js` + `.css`, hosted by hud.js as `foodShop` = Rods & Bait on the RODS tab, `baitShop` = same window on the BAIT tab, `trailShop` = Trail Shop). Walk into the Rod / Bait / Trail stall rings (`POI.foodShop / baitShop / trailShop`: **shared by everyone**, a fixed market row on the meadow side of the road, one stall opposite each leaderboard, facing south; `placeUnit` does not move them; stalls `shopStall` -> `buildFoodShop / buildBaitShop / buildTrailShop` in hub/props.js) or use the Shop window's gear tiles. The Shop window (`hud.js shop`) = gear tiles + **Boosts** (`BOOST_SHOP`: potions, Lucky Casts, Season Lures, priced `max(min, income x secs)`) + the old upgrades.
- **Rods**: still `FOODS`; 4 deep-sea rods were added after Omega (Atlantean, Leviathan's Crown, Rift, Void Eye) and `ROD_TOPS` gives some rods a head piece (trident, crown, bolt, flame, cube, eye...: `buildTop` in game/foods.js). **Rods are bought in order**: `state.rodLock(id)` = you can buy at most one tier above your best (Galaxy never waits); the card shows "Buy X first" with a lock.
- **Baits** (`BAITS`): packs of casts (`S.baits {id: n}`, `S.bait` equipped). `state.luck()` includes `baitLuck()`; `throw.js launch()` calls `state.useBait()` (one per cast; the next best owned bait goes on when a pack runs out). **Jackpot Bait** (`guarantee: 6`) makes the bite at least Secret (`rollBaddie(area, L, minRarity)`; priced from income via `dyn`). The ODDS button opens a popup with each animal rarity's chance per area for that bait (`state.oddsFor`). Bait models: `buildBait` in foods.js (`baitMesh`, `baitPortrait` in ui/snapshot.js); the real bait also hangs on the underwater hook.
- **Trails** (`TRAILS`: emitters `parts`, `ribbon` colours): `S.trails` / `S.trail`, `state.trail()` (TRY IT = `tryTrail`, five casts, not saved). `fx/trails.js emitTrail` streams them behind the bobber (`throw.js`, also on other players' throws: the `throw` message carries `trail`, backend `cleanThrow`) and behind you in the hub (`main.js hubTrail`). The Trail Shop previews are live 2D canvases (`stepPreview`).
- **Power scales with tier**: `castBlast` (rods, by `FOOD_RARITIES[].fx` 0..10 + tier) and `baitBlast` (baits, by rarity; Jackpot = gold beam, confetti, JACKPOT! text) in fx/trails.js, with `sfx.rodPower / baitUse / baitBuy / baitEquip / trailBuy / trailTry` in audio/sfx.js. Higher rods add rings, beams, a spiral, shockwaves, lightning stars, confetti; flight also sheds more sparkles.
- Save: `baits`, `bait`, `trails`, `trail` are in `fresh()` / `hydrate()` and backend `GameSave` / `sanitizeGame` (`test/GameSave.test.ts`).
- `format.js fmt` used to print 100M as "1M" (it stripped zeros from whole numbers); fixed.
